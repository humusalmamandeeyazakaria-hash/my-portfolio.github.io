About Me

I am a highly motivated and detail-oriented undergraduate Computer Science student at the University for Development Studies (UDS), passionate about leveraging software development, data analytics and mobile technology to address local and global sustainable development challenges.

My work combines practical tech stacks — Python, JavaScript and SQL — with fieldwork insight gained through the UDS Third Trimester Field Practical Programme (TTFPP), where I led digital data collection and analysis for rural community assessments. On campus, I mentor first-year students in Python through the Computer Science Students Association (COMPSSA).

I care about building software that is not just functional, but impactful and data-driven — tools that help communities and decision-makers act on evidence.
